The problem

Six-week actuarial cycles miss the loop.

Climate risk isn't a series of independent shocks. Physical damage drives premium hikes, which drive carrier exits, which drive property devaluations, which erode tax bases, which degrade the infrastructure that was supposed to reduce the physical damage. It's a Complex Adaptive System. Modelling it as a bag of independent normal distributions is why $320B in annual losses run 55%+ uninsured.

The signal Nexus surfaces
IIt > 0.65 — Stable market, business as usual.
0.40 < IIt < 0.65 — Primary insurers retreating, residual market absorbing.
IIt < 0.40 — Critical bifurcation, capital flight, mortgage backstops lost.
